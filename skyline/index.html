How to play

Skyline Stack

Stack as many floors as you can without toppling.

  1. A block slides back and forth. Tap, click or press Space to drop it.
  2. Only the part that lands on the tower stays. The overhang falls away and the next block gets narrower.
  3. Land almost exactly in line for a PERFECT drop: no trimming, and a rising chime.
  4. Three perfect drops in a row make the block grow back. Miss completely and the tower topples.
Tip: Watch the block's edges, not its middle, and tap just before they line up.

How to play

Lane Drift

Survive as long as you can and collect coins.

  1. You steer the blue arrow along three lanes. Swipe left or right, tap the left or right half of the road, or use the arrow keys.
  2. Red X blocks end your run. Every row always leaves at least one safe lane.
  3. Gold coins are worth 10 points each, and your distance scores too.
  4. The road gets faster the longer you last, up to a top speed.
Tip: Look at the lanes ahead, not at your arrow, and move early.

How to play

Moonmerge

Merge matching bodies into bigger ones. Can you reach a Galaxy?

  1. Swipe (or use the arrow keys) to slide every body on the board in that direction.
  2. When two bodies of the same size touch, they merge into the next size: Dust, Pebble, Rock, Moonlet, Moon, Dwarf planet, Planet, Gas giant, Star, Nova, Galaxy.
  3. Each merge scores points, and a new body appears after every move.
  4. If the board is full and nothing can merge, it collapses and the run ends.
Tip: Keep your biggest body in a corner and build next to it.

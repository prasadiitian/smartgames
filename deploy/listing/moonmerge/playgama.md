# Playgama submission: Moonmerge

**Title:** Moonmerge
**Genre:** Puzzle / Casual
**Languages:** English
**Platforms:** Mobile and desktop browsers, portrait and landscape
**Build:** `deploy/builds/moonmerge-v1.0.0-*.zip` (index.html at root, ~14 KB, no external requests)
**Ads/SDK:** ships with a no-op platform adapter (`platform.js`: init, gameplayStart/Stop, showRewarded, showInterstitial). Playgama Bridge is added only after account approval, as a separate commit.
**Monetization:** none at launch.

**Short description:** Swipe to merge dust into pebbles, moons, planets and stars. A calm, quick sliding merge puzzle.
**Controls:** Swipe or use arrow keys. Equal bodies merge into the next size.
**Tags:** puzzle, merge, 2048, space, casual, relaxing, singleplayer, mobile-friendly, no-ads

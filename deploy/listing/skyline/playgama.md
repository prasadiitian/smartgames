# Playgama submission: Skyline Stack

**Title:** Skyline Stack
**Genre:** Arcade / Casual
**Languages:** English
**Platforms:** Mobile and desktop browsers, portrait and landscape
**Build:** `deploy/builds/skyline-v1.0.0-*.zip` (index.html at root, ~14 KB, no external requests)
**Ads/SDK:** ships with a no-op platform adapter (`platform.js`: init, gameplayStart/Stop, showRewarded, showInterstitial). Playgama Bridge is added only after account approval, as a separate commit.
**Monetization:** none at launch.

**Short description:** Tap to drop the sliding block and build the tallest tower. Land perfectly to keep it wide.
**Controls:** Tap, click or press Space to drop. Three perfect drops in a row widen the block.
**Tags:** arcade, stacker, timing, one-button, casual, hyper-casual, singleplayer, mobile-friendly, no-ads

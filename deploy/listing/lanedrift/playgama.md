# Playgama submission: Lane Drift

**Title:** Lane Drift
**Genre:** Arcade / Casual
**Languages:** English
**Platforms:** Mobile and desktop browsers, portrait and landscape
**Build:** `deploy/builds/lanedrift-v1.0.0-*.zip` (index.html at root, ~14 KB, no external requests)
**Ads/SDK:** ships with a no-op platform adapter (`platform.js`: init, gameplayStart/Stop, showRewarded, showInterstitial). Playgama Bridge is added only after account approval, as a separate commit.
**Monetization:** none at launch.

**Short description:** Swipe between three lanes, dodge the blocks and grab coins. The road keeps getting faster.
**Controls:** Swipe or tap left / right to change lane.
**Tags:** arcade, endless-runner, reflex, one-handed, casual, hyper-casual, singleplayer, mobile-friendly, no-ads

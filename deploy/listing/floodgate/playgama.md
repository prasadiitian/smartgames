# Playgama submission: Floodgate

**Title:** Floodgate
**Genre:** Puzzle / Casual
**Languages:** English
**Platforms:** Mobile and desktop browsers, portrait and landscape
**Build:** `deploy/builds/floodgate-v1.0.0-*.zip` (index.html at root, ~14 KB, no external requests)
**Ads/SDK:** ships with a no-op platform adapter (`platform.js`: init, gameplayStart/Stop, showRewarded, showInterstitial). Playgama Bridge is added only after account approval, as a separate commit.
**Monetization:** none at launch.

**Short description:** Flood the whole board with one colour in as few moves as you can. Each level is a fresh puzzle.
**Controls:** Pick a colour to flood from the top-left corner. Finish within the move limit.
**Tags:** puzzle, flood-fill, colour, logic, casual, relaxing, singleplayer, mobile-friendly, colourblind-friendly, no-ads

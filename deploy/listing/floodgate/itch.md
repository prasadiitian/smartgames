## Description

Flood the whole board with one colour in as few moves as you can. Each level is a fresh puzzle.

How to play: Pick a colour to flood from the top-left corner. Finish within the move limit.

- Short sessions, plays well one-handed on a phone and with keyboard or mouse on desktop
- Your best score saves on your device
- Pause and mute buttons
- No sign-up, nothing to install

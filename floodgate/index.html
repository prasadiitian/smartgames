How to play

Floodgate

Turn the whole board one colour before you run out of moves.

  1. Everything starts from the top-left tile (marked with a white outline). The outlined area is your flood.
  2. Tap a colour below the board. Your flood turns that colour and swallows every touching tile of that colour.
  3. You have a limited number of moves, shown under the palette. Finish with moves to spare for more stars.
  4. Every colour also has its own shape, so you never have to rely on colour alone. Keys 1-6 work too.
Tip: Choose the colour that swallows the most tiles, and look for big patches to jump to.
